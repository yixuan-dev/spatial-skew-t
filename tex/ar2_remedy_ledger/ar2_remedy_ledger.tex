\begin{abstract}
Companion to \code{tex/ar2\_pathology\_ledger}. Two remedies were
implemented and measured on the same $40$ cells with identical seeds:
assertion-guarded reseeding (pipeline level) and the sign-anchored prior
$\lambda \sim \hn(0, 20)$ (model level). Both remove the score-relevant
pathology; their delivered scores are statistically indistinguishable
(paired over $35$ cells, $p = 0.52$ CRPS, $p = 0.28$ Brier), and the
study's headline conclusion survives both, sharpening under the prior.
The ledger closes with the untried remedies and the argument for why
their expected score gain is bounded near zero.
\end{abstract}

\section{Remedy 1 (pipeline): assertion-guarded reseeding}

Pre-registered rule, symmetric across methods. A fit failing truth
recovery (B) or predictive spread (C, $\max_h \sd(\hat y_h) \le 3 \times$
marginal SD) is refitted with seed $+\,7919\cdot\text{attempt}$, at most
three reseeds; a cell still failing is kept but flagged and excluded
from the primary analysis. Assertions use only the fit, the training
data, and the simulation truth --- never validation outcomes.

\section{Remedy 2 (model): $\lambda \sim \hn(0, 20)$}

Backend flag \code{lambda.positive} (default off; production behavior
unchanged). The joint $(\beta, \lambda)$ full conditional is multivariate
normal; under the truncated prior the exact Gibbs draw is
\begin{equation}
  \lambda \,\mid\, \cdot \;\sim\; \TN_{(0,\infty)}\!\big(m_\lambda,\, V_{\lambda\lambda}\big),
  \qquad
  \beta \,\mid\, \lambda, \cdot \;\sim\;
  N\!\big(m_\beta + V_{\beta\lambda} V_{\lambda\lambda}^{-1}(\lambda - m_\lambda),\;
        V_{\beta\beta} - V_{\beta\lambda} V_{\lambda\lambda}^{-1} V_{\lambda\beta}\big),
  \label{eq:tgibbs}
\end{equation}
with the deep-tail truncated draw by exponential rejection (Robert,
1995). No approximation is involved; the reflected branch has prior mass
zero and disappears from the posterior.

The four residual failures are, by pathology, U$\times$2 (s5 d2, both
methods; no prior can supply identification the window lacks), P
(s5 d3 m2, $\hat\lambda = 4.97$; the prior constrains the sign, not the
scale), and H (s4 d6 m2, the spread-statistic coin flip of the
pathology ledger, \S4).

\paragraph{Effect on the headline.} Under the guarded study the AR(2)
advantage at AR(2)$(0.15, 0.80)$ was CRPS $-31.3\%$ ($p = .039$); under
the HN study it is $-35.4\%$ ($p = .012$). The conclusion does not
depend on which remedy protects the study.

\paragraph{Adopted policy (31 July 2026).} From Experiment~A onward the
production pipeline uses the HN prior alone. Assertion~C is demoted to a
descriptive record --- no fit-level gate, no reseeding, no cell
exclusion --- so the primary analysis consumes all cells. The
justification is Table~\ref{tab:hn}: the prior alone delivers
guard-equivalent scores at one fit per cell, and the pathologies it
leaves (U, P, H) have no measured score path.

\section{Untried remedies and their expected value}

The score gain of any further sampler- or prior-side remedy is bounded
by an equivalence argument. Two independent mechanisms --- selection by
reseeding and removal by truncation --- already agree on the delivered
scores to within noise ($p = 0.52/0.28$); a third mechanism that removes
the same pathology can only reproduce the same healthy posterior, so its
expected score gain over the adopted policy is $\approx 0$. The value of
the entries below is therefore robustness or diagnostics, not score.

\section{What remains unprotected, quantified}

Under the adopted HN-only policy the primary analysis includes the four
residual cells. Their measured score footprint, leads 1--5:

\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
  \item \textbf{U (s5 d2, both methods).} CRPS $1.164$ vs $1.158$,
        Brier $.0194$ vs $.0190$ (i.i.d.\ vs AR(2)) --- the two methods
        agree on this dataset to three decimals, so the paired
        difference it contributes is $\approx 0$. Including it dilutes,
        never distorts.
  \item \textbf{P (s5 d3 m2).} The inflated fit scores \emph{better}
        than its healthy replacement (CRPS $1.99$ vs $2.15$), and d3 is
        an AR(2)-losing dataset under either version; inclusion is
        conservative for the headline.
  \item \textbf{H (s4 d6 m2).} Predictive cloud indistinguishable from
        the passing chains to $q_{99.9}$; scores within $1\%$ of the
        guarded version (.1338 vs .1349). No footprint.
\end{itemize}
